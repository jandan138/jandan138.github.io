The result that changes the story

A correct action can still take the wrong path.

Ordinary action accuracy mixes together systems that preserve authority and systems that guess terminal decisions directly. Authorized Path Success (APS) counts the decision and, for execution, the complete value, unit, and provenance path.

What this establishes

A model can predict a plausible action while taking an unauthorized route.

The reported benchmark is a controlled, one-author, three-template contrast set. The showcase is qualitative evidence of the same decision boundary; it is not a physics-quality score, a rollout success rate, or evidence of broad open-world generalization.
